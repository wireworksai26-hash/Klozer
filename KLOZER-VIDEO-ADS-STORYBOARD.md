## 1. Deconstruct & Pacing Blueprint (Formula Gemini Ads)

Video referensi berdurasi ~60 detik dengan ritme dinamis yang membagi narasi ke dalam **6 Fase Pacing**:

```
[00:00 - 00:05] Fase 1: The Hook & Reintroduction (UI Familiar ➔ Rebrand Sparkle)
[00:06 - 00:15] Fase 2: Kinetic Pill Actions (Kemampuan Inti Bergerak Irama Beat)
[00:16 - 00:28] Fase 3: Real-World Micro-Demos 1 (Order Cepat + Reaksi Spontan "Wow!")
[00:29 - 00:36] Fase 4: Voice & Multimodal (AI Voice Note + Bahasa Daerah)
[00:37 - 00:52] Fase 5: Deep Security & CRM Intelligence (Anti-Struk Palsu + Meta CAPI)
[00:53 - 01:00] Fase 6: Climax, Brand Slogan & Call to Action ("Welcome to Klozer Era")
```

## 5. Panduan Editing & Audio Design

1. **Music Track**:
   * Rekomendasi Genre: *Future Bass / Tech Trap Beat with energetic kicks & hi-hat rolls*.
   * BPM: 120–128 BPM.
2. **SFX Cue Points**:
   * `Detik 00:03`: Whoosh digital + Pop sound saat logo berubah.
   * `Detik 00:17`: Beep scanner barcode + Chime cash register.
   * `Detik 00:32`: Frequency swell audio wave saat VN diproses.
   * `Detik 00:40`: Laser scan sound + Shield activate thump.
   * `Detik 00:54`: Deep Sub-Bass Drop (Cinematic Braam) pada teks *"Welcome to the Klozer era"*.
3. **Pacing Transitions**:
   * Gunakan teknik **Speed Ramping** (Fast zoom-in ➔ normal speed saat demo ➔ snap zoom-out).
   * Sinkronkan setiap pergantian teks atau scene tepat pada ketukan snare/kick BGM.
